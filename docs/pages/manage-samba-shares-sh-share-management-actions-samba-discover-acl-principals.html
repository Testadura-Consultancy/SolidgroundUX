Discover domain users or groups for ACL assignment
SolidgroundUX / manage-samba-shares.sh / Share management actions / _samba_discover_acl_principals
Arguments
$1 - users or groups.

Outputs (globals):

SAMBA_ACL_USERS or SAMBA_ACL_GROUPS.
Returns
0 when at least one principal was discovered; otherwise 1.
Usage
_samba_discover_acl_principals users
